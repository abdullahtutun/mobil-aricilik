## Winter stores

- Heft hives from the back to judge their weight.
- Give fondant to colonies with low stores.

## Entrance and ventilation

- Reduce the entrance and fit a mouse guard.
- Keep the upper ventilation open.

## Varroa

Do the final treatment in the broodless period and record it with **Treatment**.
