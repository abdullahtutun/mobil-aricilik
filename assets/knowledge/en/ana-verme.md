## Preparation

- Make sure the colony is really queenless.
- Destroy any queen cells.

## Cage introduction

1. Hang the queen cage between two brood frames.
2. Leave the candy plug open; the workers release her in 2–3 days.
3. Check for eggs a week later and record it with **Inspection**.
